  User-visible:

  - **Free-H is about 2ms a frame lighter** (b1217). A scene-wide scan ran on EVERY frame to answer a
    question that changes once per scene ("is this a different girl?"); it is cached now and the plugin's
    own cost in H fell from 2.33ms to 0.30ms a frame - roughly a seventh of a 60fps frame handed back.
    Measured, not guessed: turn on *Diagnostic log* and the log carries a `CloXray PERF:` line every
    second saying what share of the frame is the plugin's.

    instantiating materials as it went, on any install whose shader zipmod does not match the DLL.

    on the stencil pair she is already using. `Ctrl+Shift+W` now does her VAGINAL womb only, and pressing
    it with a WOMB selected sets up that womb alone, taking the penis aimed nearest it - that is how a
    womb worn anywhere else, or by nobody, gets wired (b1209).
  - **Her stencil pair carries across every womb she wears** (b1206), so a second womb is not left behind.
  - **A womb away from the default spot reacts at its real depth** (b1195/b1198), and a stretched canal is
    compensated on the geometry path too (b1203). Only the womb on her vagina takes BetterPenetration's
    entry link; an anal, second or free-standing womb is entered at its own canal mouth (b1201).
  - **The penis sits in the middle of the opened canal**, not against its front wall: new vaginal wombs
    spawn 5mm forward (b1213) and womb 7.5.4 moved the canal entry marker 4mm back.
  - **Toy timing re-tuned for the re-cut rings** (b1196/b1197): the canal opens less far ahead of a toy,
    especially deep.
  - **Pairing is gentler** (b1199/b1200): the key works on the selected girl only, respects a pair that is
    already set up with her, and never removes a link.
  - **New per-womb slider `BP_IndependentRings`** (b1227, womb 7.5.5). At 0 nothing changes; above 0 each
    canal ring reacts only to what is beside it, so a small ball opens one ring at a time instead of leaving
    every ring behind it open, and the value sets how wide a band travels with the object (about 20 grips a
    ball tightly). Colliders only - a BetterPenetration penis spans every ring behind its tip either way.
    Asked for by a scene creator.
  - **A womb the key adds is wired only once it has finished starting** (b1210) - that race was silently
    skipping the collapsed uterus and the penis entry link on a freshly added womb.

- **(shaders) SKIN VEIL COVERED ONLY HER LOWER BODY - the inherited HAIR alpha clip (user-found, naked character).** CloXray_BodyRevealExtra's fragment called AlphaClip() from KKPHairDiffuse.cginc, which discards on `_AlphaMask.r * mainTex.a < 0.5`: it gates the veil on KK's CLOTHING mask, reads only the mask's R channel (the TOP/torso region), and never sees the `_alpha_a`/`_alpha_b` floats KK's own skin clip uses to mean "that garment is off, ignore its mask" - this shader does not declare them, so the game's mask push lands on nothing. A torso-shaped hole in the veil, exactly as reported. Fix: the veil is a SKIN REDRAW inside the window, so nothing may discard - `float alpha = 1.0`, both trees. `_AlphaMask` stays declared (ME row + manifest unaffected); the veil now deliberately ignores the clothing mask, which is correct inside the window. Rebuilt shader zipmods KK `6F212EAEC862` / KKS `59B13B48FEBF`; verified ONLY BodyRevealExtra's bytes changed (28 other shaders byte-identical) and the womb embeds none of it, so no womb rebuild.

- **(analysis) RENDER-QUEUE RE-BASE BELOW 2500: REJECTED, reason recorded.** Clo15 measured the male's own penis material: shaded at queue 2500, washed-out at 2501 - Unity's opaque/transparent cutoff, above which geometry no longer receives the screen-space shadow buffer. That is why the in-window redraw (3502) looks flat: same shader, same textures, later pipeline stage, NOT a material defect. Re-basing the stack into 2476..2500 was investigated and rejected: hard floor at 2475 (xukmi OutlineAlpha2 does a full-byte `Fail DecrSat` there and would corrupt our region), the skybox draws AFTER the opaque queue, every ordinary scene transparent in 2501..3499 would flip from under to over the window, the move is all-or-nothing (the penis carve+redraw cannot go alone), and it breaks saved scenes' persisted queues plus the documented 3490 machine-plane contract. If the look is ever worth fixing: a shadow-modulate pass after the redraw, or per-character compensation in ME. Queues stay as they are.

- **1004 — ONE-KEY SETUP TAKES THE PENIS OVER FROM A STRAY CONSTRAINT (user-found).** `Ctrl+Shift+W` set the womb up but left the penis unshaded and unaimed; the log said "no penetrator found" while the womb's own pairing captured the same male seconds later. Cause: `NearestPenisEnd` skips any penis whose `k_f_dan_end` is held by a NodesConstraint other than this womb's `penis_target` (the guard that stops two wombs fighting over one male) - the scene had a hand-made `cf_j_spine02 -> k_f_dan_end` link, so the penis was found and silently rejected. The one-key hotkey promises a finished scene, so it now TAKES OVER: `AutoBodyReveal.PenisTakeover` (set only around the W hotkey's ApplyAll, never for `Shift+Alt+X` or the load path) lets the search accept a foreign-held penis, and once the couple is settled - nearest, in range, not another womb's - `NodeConstraintBridge.ReleaseNodeFromForeignHolders` deletes the foreign link via NC's `RemoveConstraintAt`. **A link parented to any `penis_target` is never removed**, so another couple's aim (and our own) is untouchable, and every removal is logged as a warning because it destroys a user-made constraint.

- **1002 — GARMENT GHOST (requested feature): clothes over the window now ghost translucently instead of being cut through.** A second per-garment ME copy on **CloXray/BodyRevealExtra** - the SAME shader the skin veil uses (user decision), so no new shader, no zipmod rebuild: the clone keeps the garment's own textures/colors, the shader brings the window stencil gate and the `XrayAlpha` fade. Queue **3505** (one past the skin veil - fabric is outermost, same-queue ties are sorting luck). Default XrayAlpha **0.7** (~30% of the fabric still visible; the skin veil stays 0.9/~10%, tuned separately by eye), written ONCE at create - the per-garment ME slider is the user's afterwards. `EnsureGarmentVeil` runs from both clothes branches and both accessory branches, so already-stamped scenes gain the veil on the next apply and pair cycling keeps `StencilBody_Plus_1` in sync. Perf: +1 draw call per garment material, fragments outside the window die at early stencil; the skinning runs once regardless. Known edge to test: the ME last-submesh binding quirk now has a second copy per garment to hit it with.

- **1001 — TESS-BODY FIX (GitHub issue #3, reproduced locally and mechanism confirmed by the StampZTest=8 test).** KKUTS_Tess-family shaders tessellate and DISPLACE the skin in front of our flat BodyReveal stamp copy, so the stamp's `ZTest LEqual` fails up close and the window never opens; zoomed out the distance-adaptive tessellation (`_TessEdgeLength`) collapses to the base mesh and it works — hence the reporter's "visible only when zoomed out". Fix: new shader float **`_StampPull`** (BodyReveal, default **0** = bit-identical legacy path, ME row added) pulls the stamp vertices toward the camera; the plugin sets it to **10mm ONLY when the source material's shader name contains "tess"** (body + garment stamps, `ApplyStampPull`), fail-loud if the installed shader zipmod predates the property. Occlusion by props and hands survives (their margin is cm-scale, the pull is mm-scale) — the `StampZTest=8` hammer, which forfeits that occlusion, stays a manual escape hatch only. Alternatives considered and rejected: masking tess displacement regionally (their shader has no mask input), zeroing the tess props (destroys the look the user chose), a tessellating stamp variant (version-fragile parity with every KKUTS release). Shader zipmods rebuilt (KK `AA0B55ECAA15` / KKS `FB60CC2DA9DE`); womb rebuild NOT needed — verified the three embedded shaders' raw bytes are identical to shipped (only BodyReveal changed, which the womb does not embed).

- **966-969** — X-RAY MACHINE REGRESSION properly closed (b965's `_LimbBlockMask_159on_31off` was the wrong cure and is REMOVED again). The real breaker: b929's limb pass gave the garment stamps `_StampLimbGate` default 128 = stamp only where bit7 is already set — but the machine plane IS the bit7 writer, so machine scenes could never satisfy it. Fixes, in order: `_StampLimbGate` default 0 with the plugin setting 128 explicitly on its garment/accessory copies (rule recorded: a shader default must never assume the plugin configured the material); then the clean redesign — hand/limb blocking moved OFF bit7 entirely onto a low-5 REGION SENTINEL (31): the body material's `LimbBlock` pass (after FORWARD, queue 2500) writes region 31 on limb pixels, garment stamp copies run at queue **2499** so the body's own stamp wins ties, and the Organ wipe passes went back to `ReadMask 31`. Region 31 never punches (punch passes demand `Comp Equal` on the pair values) and the two `Comp LEqual` DepthWrite passes PASS at 31 but die on ZTest vs unpunched depth — depth blocks them, not stencil (the b969 comments first claimed the reverse; corrected, also in XRAY_RENDER_MODEL §8b). Bit7 is now RESERVED for user machine windows: old machine scenes work with NOTHING set, and the user's scene confirmed on screen. 31 chosen over 19 for headroom (pairs stop at 16/17; 19-30 stay free).
 - user-reported regression: organ items stopped showing through a machine window that worked before 1.1.2. The cause was mine, not the womb. Those scenes mark their reveal window with stencil **bit7** (the plane stamps `StencilRef 128` / `WriteMask 128` at queue 3490; the organ items run `StencilBody 132/133` with `StencilReadMask 159`; the cum uses `CumStencilRef 192`), and b929 gave bit7 the OPPOSITE meaning for hand/limb blocking, hardcoding `ReadMask 159` into CloXray_Organ's two `OutBodyBack*` wipe passes so they fire only where bit7 is CLEAR - inside the window it is SET, so the out-of-body organ path was suppressed exactly where the scene wants organs. Diagnosed by reading the scene file's MaterialEditor block (shader list + saved floats), and the womb was cleared by comparing `penis_target` / `penis_target2` / `clo_canal_entry` byte-for-byte against the pre-7.4.1 build. Fix (user chose option B, so the plugin does no runtime work): the gate is now the property **`_LimbBlockMask_159on_31off`**, default **159** (normal play unchanged, the womb needs nothing set) and settable to **31** per organ material in a machine scene - one edit, saved with the scene. Rejected the alternative of flipping bit7 to mean 'reveal allowed' everywhere: her body stamp would then set it across her whole non-limb silhouette, so in a machine scene her torso would satisfy the organs' condition and the window would stop being a window - and the outline pass already borrows bit7 per frame as a scratch flag.
